# Welcome to Spidercrab

**Spidercrab turns your iPad into a hands-on remote for REAPER.** Launch clips, play and record, tweak effects, mute and solo tracks, and drop in samples — all by touch, from across the room.

Think of it as a control surface you already own: instead of reaching for the mouse, you tap, hold, and slide right on the iPad screen.

## What you can do

- **Mix by touch** — volume, pan, mute, solo, and record-arm on every track.
- **Play & record** — transport controls and clip recording from the couch.
- **Shape your sound** — add effects, load effect chains, and tweak parameters with big finger-friendly sliders.
- **Work with samples** — browse, preview, tag, and drop samples onto tracks or clip slots.
- **Launch clips** — a session grid for triggering loops and scenes (works with Playtime 2).
- **Turn clips into instruments** — send a sample or clip to a sampler and play it across the keys.

## What you'll need

- **REAPER** running on your computer, with the Spidercrab extension installed.
- An **iPad** (or any tablet) on the **same Wi-Fi network** as the computer.
- For the clip launcher: **Playtime 2** (via Helgobox).

## How you get to it

There's no App Store download — Spidercrab is a control surface that **REAPER serves to your iPad's browser**. You install it into REAPER once, then open a web address in Safari (and can add it to your home screen so it feels like a real app). The full walkthrough is in [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

Once you're in, everything happens by touch — head to [Touch Gestures](gestures.md) to learn the moves.
