# Settings ⚙️

Connection status, appearance, and a bit of housekeeping.

## Connection

Shows whether the app is connected to REAPER. If it ever drops, this is where you'll see it.

- **Refresh Tracks** — re-read your project if the track list looks out of date.

## Appearance

- **Theme** — Light, Dark, or match your iPad's system setting. Your choice is remembered.
- **Tab Bar Position** — put the tabs along the **top** or the **bottom** of the screen, whichever's comfier to reach.

## Samples & effects housekeeping

- **Refresh Plugin List** — rebuild the list of installed effects after you add a new plugin to your system.
- **Refresh Chain Cache** — rebuild the list of saved effect chains.
- **FXChains folder** — tell Spidercrab where your REAPER effect chains live (for example `…\REAPER\FXChains`) so it can find them.
- **Sample folders** — add one or more folders that hold your samples. Each one has a **scan** button (to index it for fast browsing) and a **remove** button. These are what the [Media](media.md) tab browses.

## Playtime

- **Launch Playtime** — start Playtime 2 without leaving the app, so the [Clip Launcher](playtime.md) is ready to go.

## Good to know

After the app itself is updated on the computer, give the iPad page a **hard refresh** (pull down to reload) so it picks up the new version.
