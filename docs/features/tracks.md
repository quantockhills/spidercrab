# Tracks & Mixing 🎚️

This is home base — the tab the app opens on. Up top you've got transport (play, record, stop); below that, a card for every track in your project.

## Playing and recording

The buttons at the top control REAPER's transport:

- **Play** — start playback
- **Record** — arm and roll recording
- **Stop** — stop

Whatever you press here matches REAPER exactly, and the buttons light up to show what's happening.

## Mixing a track

Each track has its own card with everything you need to balance a mix:

- **Volume** — slide the fader up and down
- **Pan** — place the track left or right
- **Mute** — silence the track
- **Solo** — hear it on its own
- **Arm** — get it ready to record
- **Record mode** — record **audio** or **MIDI** (overdub or replace); Spidercrab sets up the right record input for you

**Tap a track's card to select it** in REAPER. Add a new track with the **Add Track** button, and **collapse** a card to tidy up the view when a track's got a lot going on.

Everything you do here happens in REAPER instantly — and if a fader moves on the desktop or on another iPad, your view updates to match. No refreshing.

## Effects on a track

Each card also shows that track's effects as little cards, with an **Add FX** button. You can do a lot without leaving this tab:

- **Double-tap an effect** to switch it off and on (bypass) — a bypassed effect dims and greys out so you can see at a glance what's off.
- **Hold an effect** (about half a second) to arm it for removal — the card flips to a red **"Delete? ✕"** confirmation. Tap it again to actually delete, or tap anywhere else to cancel.
- **Tap the small ▼ in the card's corner** to open its parameters in an **inline drawer** — tweak the knobs right here on the track card, no need to visit the FX tab. The drawer also lets you **step through presets** (◀ ▶) and **pin** your favourite knobs to a "Pinned" strip up top so they're always in reach (your pins are remembered).
- **Drag an individual effect** to reorder it on its own, even if it's inside a chain block — or **drag a whole chain block** (by its green header) to move every effect in it together.
- **Hold a chain block's header for 2 seconds** to open the **Chain Cycler** — a little pop-up that steps **◀ / ▶** through the other chains in that folder (showing how many effects each has) so you can swap the whole chain out and tap **Done**.
- **Tap Add FX** to add an effect.
- **Hold Add FX** to open a quick search — start typing and it finds plugins *and* saved effect chains; tap one to drop it on the track.

**Tidy up:** use **Collapse** (top of the tab) to fold all the track cards down to a summary when things get busy, and **Expand** to bring them back.

For everything you can do with effects and their knobs, see [Effects (FX)](fx.md). Not sure what tap-vs-hold does anywhere? The full list is in [Touch Gestures](../gestures.md).
